Flow

  1. ① Track papers. The researcher adds a paper by PDF or DOI; Storage finds the DOI (GROBID), fills metadata (Crossref/OpenAlex) and keeps a PDF (OpenAlex, then Semantic Scholar).
  2. ⑤ Poll. Every 24 h Updating reads the paper list, looks each DOI up in Crossref and OpenAlex, and stores a snapshot in Storage.
  3. ⑥ Nudge. When a paper's snapshot changed, Updating sends Research Evaluation just its id: POST /evaluate/changes {paper_ids}.
  4. ⑦ Review changes. Research Evaluation compares recent snapshots, skips changes it already has, and stores each new one as an alert with a severity and recommendation.
  5. ⑧ Investigate. In the background it opens a report for the new alerts and stores the evidence: notices, new versions and the current copy (Crossref, Europe PMC), whose PDFs Storage downloads.
  6. ⑨ Assess impact. Gemini reads the paper, the evidence and the researcher's draft, and the evaluation is written into the report.
  7. ② ③ Act. The researcher sees the alerts and reports, acknowledges or dismisses alerts and keeps notes.
  8. ④ Draft. The researcher's own draft, one per project, is what ⑨ judges the change against.
